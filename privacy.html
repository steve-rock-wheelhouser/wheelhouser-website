Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Ironclad Privacy Charter

  • We Never Track You: Zero hidden telemetry beacons, usage surveillance, keystroke logging, or cross-app fingerprinting.
  • We Never Collect, Use, Mine, or Sell Customer Data: Your files, prompts, workflows, and operational records never leave your hardware unless you explicitly configure an optional third-party action.
  • We Never Push Ads: 100% ad-free software—no ad networks, sponsored clutter, or dark-pattern interruptions shoved into your experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wheelhouser LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our website, native desktop applications, and custom engineering services.

General Principles — Zero Telemetry by Design

At Wheelhouser LLC, we engineer sovereign software with privacy by design. Our desktop applications (including the Core Workstation Suite and Visual AI utilities) execute locally on your workstation hardware. We do not embed background analytics, keystroke telemetry, or cloud document scraping into our native desktop software.

Information Collected via Optional Integrations or Inquiries

When you voluntarily submit a custom desktop app brief or enable third-party publishing integrations (such as the Meta Platform integration in Marquee-Magic), we may process only the information required to fulfill that request:

  • Contact details you provide directly (name, organization, email, phone number, and project scope).
  • When authorized in Marquee-Magic: public profile metadata, managed Facebook Pages / Instagram Business Accounts, and OAuth access tokens required strictly to publish content you initiate.

How We Use Your Information

We use any information you voluntarily provide solely to:

  • Respond to custom software architecture inquiries and fulfill contracted engineering engagements.
  • Authenticate optional third-party social publishing workflows that you explicitly configure.
  • Maintain cryptographic package repositories and software update channels.

We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or organizational data with third parties.

Data Security

All software distributions are signed with 4096-bit RSA GPG keys, Microsoft Store / Authenticode certificates, or Apple notarization credentials. Any optional integration tokens remain stored locally on your authorized workstation.
